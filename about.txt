Libiry gives you an overview of all your analog, digital and audiobooks. And if you want to add book summaries to your overview, you can do that too!
It works with your own book folder structure. There's no separate database.

Analog books can be either:
- Stored in a multi-book markdown file (one file per book shelf), or
- Stored in separate markdown files for every book

You can choose your own markdown field names. 

You only need one line per book in order for the tool to work.
In it, you store either a URL to the cover or the book title.
This means you can redo your stock taking quickly when your overview is out of date.
Taking stock of your analog books is not a big project. It can be done in parts, for isbn codes, only to find out your book does not exist in the database.

Libiry visualizes ALL of your books and zines, with or without an ISBN number.
It uses three databases:
Open Library
Google Books
Europeana
If an ISBN was found, but not in those databases, a link to the Worldcat catalog is provided.
If no title could be identified, the document title is shown. 

And all it takes is one photo per book shelf.

For a quick start:
Take a picture of every book shelf with https://sappelen.github.io/BookSpineScanner.
Place the resulting markdown files in your e-library.
If you want to, put your book summaries in there as well.

You now have an overview of all your books.
